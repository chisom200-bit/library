About the library

A home for ideas worth sharing

Research, reflection and everyday learning, written clearly and given away freely.

This library exists because ideas grow when they are shared. Every document here began as a question, a piece of research, a lesson learned the hard way or a thought worth writing down, and each one is published openly so that anyone, anywhere, can read it without signing in or paying.

It is a space for lifelong learning: curiosity over certainty, understanding over memorisation, and reflection over reaction. Whether you are a student, a professional or simply someone who enjoys learning, you are welcome to read, download, share and think alongside it.

What guides the library

Six principles behind every page

The standards every document and post is held to.

  1. 01

    Open by default

    No accounts, no paywalls and no hoops to jump through. If it is published, anyone can read and download it.

  2. 02

    Clarity over complexity

    Big ideas explained in plain words. If a sentence needs a dictionary, it gets rewritten.

  3. 03

    Honest about uncertainty

    Claims are separated from evidence, and what is not yet known is said out loud.

  4. 04

    Learning, not performing

    The aim is understanding and growth, not impressing anyone, the author included.

  5. 05

    Careful with sources

    Ideas are credited, facts are checked and opinions are clearly labelled as opinions.

  6. 06

    Always improving

    Documents and posts are revised when better information arrives, and corrections are welcome.

How it works

From question to shared knowledge

  1. 1

    Research & reflect

    A question is explored through reading, study and honest reflection until it is clear enough to explain.

  2. 2

    Write & publish

    The result is written up as a PDF document or a short blog post and added to the library.

  3. 3

    Read, download & share

    Anyone can read online, download for offline study, or pass a link to someone who might need it.

Knowledge grows when it is given away. The more it is shared, the more of it there is.

Questions you may have

Is everything really free?

Yes. Every document can be read online or downloaded as a PDF without signing in, creating an account or paying.

Can I share or print the documents?

Please do. Share the link freely and print copies for personal study or teaching. If you quote a document, please credit the library.

Can I republish the content elsewhere?

Please get in touch first. Documents are shared for reading and learning, not for resale or republication without permission.

How often is the library updated?

New documents and blog posts are added over time. The most recent work always appears at the top of the home page.

Can I suggest a topic or report a mistake?

Yes, and it is appreciated. Use the contact address in the footer.
