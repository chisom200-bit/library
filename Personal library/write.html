Blog Studio

Write, upload, edit and remove posts

Fill in the form (or upload a write-up and a cover image) and your post appears on your blog straight away as a private preview.

How publishing works. This is a static website, so a web page cannot store posts for every visitor by itself. Instead:

  1. Add, edit, hide or remove posts below. Click Preview on blog to see them on your real blog pages (only you see this).
  2. When you are happy, click Download posts.js and replace the file in your project folder.
  3. Redeploy (git push, or drag the folder onto Netlify). The blog then updates for everyone.

Your work-in-progress is kept in this browser only, so download before you switch devices or clear your browser data.
